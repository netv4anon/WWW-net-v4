# Source, historical-notice and scope clarifications

The original NET-V4 archives retain historical notices verbatim because they form part of the source record. A placeholder Zenodo DOI in those historical notices is **not a real deposit or access address**. Placeholder author fields are not current attribution. The inherited EMS endpoint attribution is incorrect: the inspected builder identifies NYC DOT ATR `7ym2-wayt` and FDNY Fire Incident Dispatch `8m42-w767`. Read the supplied `SOURCE_ATTRIBUTION.md` and construction documentation. Do not cite the historical placeholder DOI or infer a broader license from it.

The original constructed events are synthetic and were not calibrated against measured incident dynamics. Observation-tagged controls may have been processed. Numeric input slots and profile-similarity reference sequences are not verified neighboring roads. Recorded-year splits do not establish source-independent observation periods. The original 12-to-12 task is preserved; the separately identified MV extension does not replace it.

Basel-Stadt dataset 100006 is supplied with source metadata under CC BY 4.0 and publisher attribution. Hourly counts are divided by four to form assumed within-hour values. These are not observed quarter-hour trajectories. All 73 admitted profiles and all failed/heterogeneous checkpoint results remain included. The later old-profile sensitivity is post-confirmation analysis, not a replacement independent confirmation.

The paired hourly averaging is computed on float32 profiles. A historical float32 summation check produced zero discrepancy; float64 re-accumulation instead gives maximum absolute discrepancy 0.00048828125 and maximum relative discrepancy 1.1850625707112023e-7. The main paper uses the latter result. No stored profile or forecast was changed to obtain this correction.

Upstream model code keeps its MIT/Apache notices, including required copyright attribution. NYC source terms and retained component declarations remain separate; the resource does not grant rights to redistribute every upstream dataset under a single new license. The contextual-model source prefix has limited coverage and is not the complete upstream TrafficFresh release. The IGSTGNN raw source is not bundled and must be acquired separately under its source conditions. Saved outputs and the exact source digest are included.

Anonymization changes only personal machine-path strings and distribution documentation. Original scientific freeze objects can retain non-identifying historical archive paths. The executable review entry points use package-relative paths and new output directories. Historical preregistration/selection records remain historical records; current distribution inventories are not new preregistrations.
